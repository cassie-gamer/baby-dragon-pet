# Baby Dragon Pet - Toothless! 🐉🖤

A cute pet care game starring **Toothless**, the baby Night Fury from How to Train Your Dragon!

## The Story

You find a mysterious black egg in the forest. Tap it 3 times... CRACK! Out hatches baby Toothless. Now he's yours to take care of!

## How to Play

- **Feed Fish** - Toothless LOVES fish! Keeps his Hunger bar full.
- **Play** - Zoom around together! Fills his Fun bar (but makes him a little tired).
- **Sleep** - Let him curl up for a cozy nap. Fully restores Energy.
- **Scratch Ears** - His favorite spot! A little happiness boost.

Keep his Hunger, Fun and Energy bars full, and watch him grow from a tiny hatchling into a big Night Fury!

## Unity Setup

1. Create a 2D or 3D scene with your Toothless character
2. Add `ToothlessPet.cs` to Toothless, hook up the UI bars and mood text
3. Add `NightFuryEgg.cs` to the egg, drag baby Toothless into the slot (starts hidden)
4. Add `ToothlessGrowth.cs` to Toothless, drag the ToothlessPet into the slot
5. Make UI buttons that call FeedFish(), Play(), Sleep(), and ScratchEars()

Have fun with your dragon! 🐉
